# Invariants: the Bank and budget

What must always hold where tokens come from and where they go: the Bank (`packages/core/src/bank.ts`, `world.bank`), the budget (`packages/core/src/budget.ts`, `world.budget`: `Budget.canStart`, `Budget.charge`), the finance summary (`packages/core/src/finance.ts`) and the runtime's funding and charging (`fundingFor`, `chargeUsage` in `packages/runtime/src/session.ts`). Each invariant names the test that proves it. The design is in [docs/bank.md](../bank.md), and the map is in [docs/architecture.md](../architecture.md#map-the-system-in-10-minutes).

**Citation format** (checked by `npm run check:docs`): each invariant has one or more lines `- Test: \`<test file>\` › \`<it text>\``, with the `it` text copied exactly. See [grants.md](grants.md) for details.

## Invariants

1. **Every figure is in adjusted output tokens, and a charge books exactly the session's `adjustedOutputTokens`.** Each token kind is weighted by its list price over the model's output price. The usage ledger, the Bank's debits and the allocation all carry the same five counters, kind by kind.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/adjusted.test.ts` › `weights each kind by its list price over the model's output price, per family`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/token-counters.test.ts` › `a bare number is already adjusted; a session's charge books exactly its adjustedOutputTokens`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/token-counters.test.ts` › `a charge by kind books its adjusted output tokens, with the same counters, into the usage ledger, the Bank's debits and the allocation`
2. **Conservation: every charge for a funded building is booked in full.** What a charge adds to the usage ledger equals what it adds to Bank debits plus allocation spend. Nothing is dropped when a source runs out: the source that takes the rest takes all of it (there is no hard stop, see 6). The one exception is an agent with no building to draw from (`recordUsage` alone); a building with nothing left is still debited (it overshoots, invariant 6), never left as "Not funded" (invariant 13).
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank-invariants.test.ts` › `every charge is booked in full as Bank debits plus allocation spend, and the finance identity holds after each one`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/budget.test.ts` › `recordUsage counts unfunded usage (no allocation drawn) and still bumps the agent's counters`
3. **The finance identity holds exactly: `used + expired + left = total + overdrawn`.** `total` is credits plus donations, and `overdrawn` is usage that nothing received has paid for yet. `GET /api/finance` reports it as `sumCheck`, and the Bank page (Tokens section) shows it red when it fails.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/finance.test.ts` › `an overdrawn durable pool keeps left at 0 and books the debt as overdrawn, so the identity balances`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/finance.test.ts` › `balances on a real Bank: subscription credit, overshoot past the durable share, a donation`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank-invariants.test.ts` › `every charge is booked in full as Bank debits plus allocation spend, and the finance identity holds after each one`
4. **A recurring input credits once per renewal. Renewals missed while the server was down are paid once, not once per missed period**, and the next renewal moves past now. A subscription credits min(daily quota, weekly cap left).
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank.test.ts` › `a recurring input credits only on its renewal date, once per renewal`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank.test.ts` › `a subscription is admin-only and credits min(daily quota, weekly cap left) each day`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank-invariants.test.ts` › `after downtime a renewal credits once, and a subscription's catch-up credit expires at the next renewal after now`
5. **Subscription credits expire at the next renewal and don't roll over.** A draw takes expiring credits first (soonest to expire first), then durable ones. What wasn't drawn when a credit expires is counted as `expired`.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank.test.ts` › `every building draws the one general balance: expiring subscription credits first, then durable; expiring ones don't roll over`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/finance.test.ts` › `a subscription credit partly drawn is left until it expires, then its undrawn rest is expired`
6. **There is no hard stop, and every balance shown is floored at 0.** A session may overshoot: the Bank's durable balance goes negative, and that debt is paid back from the next credits before anything new can be spent. `balance`, `remaining`, the summary's `balanceTokens` and finance `left` never go below 0. The debt shows as `overdrawn`, and `fundingFor` returns null while it is at or below 0.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank-invariants.test.ts` › `balances shown are floored at 0 after an overshoot; the debt stays in the pool's durable share and the next credit pays it back first`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank.test.ts` › `every building draws the one general balance: expiring subscription credits first, then durable; expiring ones don't roll over`
7. **One general balance, no pools; the Town Hall draws like any building.** Every building (the Town Hall included, 1:1 mt_cz11aqe81a) draws the same balance, first come, first served; earmarks are the only per-subtree funding (pools were deleted, docs/how-work-gets-done.md §7; migration 067 leaves their tables unread).
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank.test.ts` › `buildings share the balance first come first served; the Town Hall draws like any building`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank.test.ts` › `leaves the pools-era tables and columns in place, read by nothing; debits still get the old column's default`
7a. **Free tokens: claims are paid where their sessions would draw.** Funded grants' remaining, open proposals' asks and open reservations each consume the nearest earmark above their goal first, then the shared general balance; free tokens under a goal = what is left of the general balance + of the earmarks at or above it, never below 0.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/free-tokens.test.ts` › `claims are paid by the nearest earmark above their goal first, then the general balance, which every goal shares`
7c. **The operating reserve (town `operatingReserveTokens`, 1:1 mt_lb7tm75jgb) is never free: it comes off the general balance before any claim; grant funding past what is free is refused while it is above 0; sessions still draw on it.**
   - Test: `packages/core/test/free-tokens.test.ts` › `comes off the general balance before any claim; earmarks are untouched; never below 0`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/free-tokens.test.ts` › `funding a grant (or topping one up) is refused when it would dip into the reserve; a reserve of 0 keeps the old rule`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/free-tokens.test.ts` › `directed work, 1:1s and meetings still draw on the balance below the reserve`
7b. **An exploration reservation claims its chunk less what its sessions spent while it is open, and closes (the rest returns) once its assignment is no longer pending or active** (the reservations table, `packages/core/src/explore.ts`, migration 068).
   - Test: `packages/core/test/explore.test.ts` › `the tick reserves one chunk and queues one explorer on the building queue; the reservation claims free tokens`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/explore.test.ts` › `closes the reservation when its work ends (spent booked, rest returns), then waits 30 min before the next explorer`
8. **Funding order is fixed: a member-funded grant's own input first, then the nearest earmark above the work's goal that covers one expected session, then subscription, the building's Bank share, donor-key, and api-key.** Every session runs on exactly one credential the Bank holds (a subscription input's token, an api-key input's key, a donor's key); a Bank with none holds the work, and a credential in the host env is a hard failure (post-mortem 2026-10-30).
   - Test: `packages/core/test/member-funding.test.ts` › `a session on the grant is paid from the input first, then the pool; each debit names the input`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/streams.test.ts` › `picks the nearest earmark above the work's goal that covers a session, then charges it first`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/budget.test.ts` › `funding order: subscription → donor-key → api-key; the fallback is the first donor-key / api-key allocation`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/bank.test.ts` › `charge draws from the general balance before the building's allocations and keeps the building able to start`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/bank-fallback.test.ts` › `a subscription-funded building runs on the subscription input's token: one credential, no fallback key beside it`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/bank-fallback.test.ts` › `nothing in the Bank: held with NO_KEY_HOLD; a subscription input without its token: NO_TOKEN_HOLD (never the host env)`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/secrets.test.ts` › `a credential in the host env is a second auth source and a hard failure (post-mortem 2026-10-30: the env's key was billed for eight days)`
9. **One credential per session, and the Bank books the one it handed out.** The env a session starts with (host `sessionEnv`, desk `deskEnv`) carries exactly one credential variable and no failover key beside it (`assertOneCredential`); a session that dies on the subscription's cap ends there, and the *next* session is the one that runs on the Bank's API key (`Budget.failoverFunding`, while `Bank.capState().cappedAt` is set), booked to that key. The session row records the credential it was handed (`funding`, `credentialInputId`) at start; a desk claiming a different funding at the end, or an old image's in-session failover, is an `alert: funding-mismatch`, never a rebooking (1:1 mt_x40qq8poc3).
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/secrets.test.ts` › `every session env carries exactly one credential variable: two, none, or a failover key beside one is a hard failure (1:1 mt_x40qq8poc3)`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/desk.test.ts` › `the desk env carries exactly one credential (the token) and no fallback key; the session row books the credential handed out`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/desk.test.ts` › `the Bank books what it handed out, not the desk's word: a desk claiming api-key (or an old image's in-session failover) is an alert, the session stays subscription`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/bank-fallback.test.ts` › `failover is the next session's pick: after a cap death the Bank hands out the api-key input's key (booked as api-key) until the cap resets; never two in one session (1:1 mt_x40qq8poc3)`
   - Test: `packages/desk/test/run.test.ts` › `a spend-limit error ends the session with the error and spendLimit; no rerun on the fallback key, which never reaches the SDK`
10. **Every day's funding is reconciled against the Bank's own records, and what the Bank can't explain is an alert.** Once per UTC day (`reconcileDue`, runtime tick), the day before is checked (`reconcileFunding`, `packages/core/src/reconcile.ts`): every api-key session ran on a Bank input, and no desk claimed a funding it wasn't handed. One `system` event per day, with `alert: funding-reconcile` when it isn't; the HUD shows funding alerts for a day. The check never leaves the town: it reads no Anthropic Console or Admin API and needs no secret (Mitchell, 2026-10-31: the project does not touch anyone's Console).
   - Test: `packages/core/test/reconcile.test.ts` › `a day whose sessions all ran on credentials the Bank lists is OK, and the result carries nothing from outside the town`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/reconcile.test.ts` › `an api-key session on a key the Bank doesn't list, or a desk's funding claim that didn't match, is an alert`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/reconcile.test.ts` › `a fresh town with no sessions yesterday stays quiet; a day with a session is reconciled once`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/reconcile.test.ts` › `the reconciliation never leaves the town: no fetch, no Console or Admin API URL, no secret read (Mitchell, 2026-10-31)`
11. **Exhaustion stops new starts and is undone only by new tokens.** When a building's budget runs out, `out-of-budget` is set once with one `budget.exhausted` event. A new donation resumes work. There is no per-agent or family daily cap (deleted 2026-10-31, 1:1 mt_q10xf3bkoq): `dailyTokensPerAgent` is read by nothing.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/budget.test.ts` › `no per-agent daily cap (1:1 mt_q10xf3bkoq): a resident past dailyTokensPerAgent keeps starting; resetDaily zeroes today`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/budget.test.ts` › `building exhaustion stops all residents until a new donation; allocations are charged in draw order (donor key before the town key)`
12. **A cap event never holds starts by itself.** The Bank learns no daily cap from where sessions died (removed 2026-11-01). A `bank.cap` is observed (`capState().cappedAt`, a death) and only changes which credential the next session picks.
    - Test: `packages/core/test/caps.test.ts` › `a cap event is observed (cappedAt, a death) but holds nothing: the assignment may still start, no bank.hold`
13. **Every token a session uses is debited, as it runs and however it ends** (1:1 mt_x40qq8poc3 decision 2). The session row's counters are what the Bank has debited for it: a running session's usage (the host's `UsageMeter` over the SDK's assistant messages, a desk's `usage` activity posts) is booked at most once a minute (`bookRunning`: row and debit in one transaction, so `Budget.fundingFor`'s next pick sees the real balance; the out-of-budget check waits for the end), and the end (`endAndCharge` → `settleUsage`) trues up to the final figure, never below what was booked (`mergeUsage`). A session that crashes before its result, dies on the spend limit, is stopped, loses its desk or is orphaned by a restart keeps every booked token, and its debits equal its row's `adjustedOutputTokens`.
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/running-debit.test.ts` › `each new call is booked at once; the result trues up; debits = the session's usage; a repeated block counts once`
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/running-debit.test.ts` › `a session that crashes mid-turn (no result) still counts and debits every call it made`
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/running-debit.test.ts` › `a lost desk (exits with no session-end) keeps what its posts booked`
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/running-debit.test.ts` › `a spend-limit death with a smaller (or no) figure than was posted never books less than the posts; its assignment goes back to the front of the queue (no fallback report)`
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/running-debit.test.ts` › `a restart orphans the session: its row keeps what was booked and the debits still match`
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/running-debit.test.ts` › `a building with nothing left is still debited (overshoot), so the Spending tab's Not funded doesn't grow`
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/running-debit.test.ts` › `its residents' usage is debited to its pool (General): no usage-only path, nobody flipped out-of-budget while the pool has tokens`
    - Test: `packages/desk/test/run.test.ts` › `posts its running usage for the Bank to book, and a session that dies before its result still ends with every call (1:1 mt_x40qq8poc3 decision 2)`

14. **Availability is observed per Bank input and model class, never predicted** (docs/bank.md "Promised and available"). A session's error that is about its credential records a limit on the input it ran on: one model class for a model limit, every model for a plan window, billing or auth. The limit holds until the reset the provider named, or until a probe time that backs off (1 h doubling to 24 h; 6 h doubling to 48 h for billing and auth). A session that succeeds on the input and model clears it. Errors that aren't about the credential (overloaded, a transient 429) record nothing.
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `reads what the provider said by what it means for the credential`
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `with no reset named, the next try backs off: 1 h doubling to 24 h for limits, 6 h doubling to 48 h for billing and auth`
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `an Opus success clears nothing for Fable; a Fable success (the probe) clears the Fable limit`
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `no reset known: after probeAt one session may try; failing again doubles the wait`
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `rate_limit_event: rejected records the named window with its reset; allowed clears it`
15. **Dispatch never runs a session on a credential limited for its model, and never switches the model.** `Budget.fundingFor` takes the session's model. It skips a limited input for another credential that serves the same model, or holds the work with the limit's words (map line to the Bank). A building-queue row is checked on its own model, not its manager's.
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `a Fable limit holds Fable work with its reason; Opus work on the same subscription keeps running`
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `fails over only to a key that serves the same model; never to another model`
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `billing and auth: a broken key is skipped for every model and tried again after 6 h`
    - Test: `packages/core/test/availability.test.ts` › `a plan window limits every model until the reset it names; then work runs again`
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/bank-fallback.test.ts` › `failover is the next session's pick: after a cap death the Bank hands out the api-key input's key (booked as api-key) until the cap resets; never two in one session (1:1 mt_x40qq8poc3)`
16. **A session that dies on its credential doesn't fail its work.** Its assignment goes back to the front of the queue, with no fallback report, to be funded by another credential or to wait with the limit's reason.
    - Test: `packages/runtime/test/running-debit.test.ts` › `a spend-limit death with a smaller (or no) figure than was posted never books less than the posts; its assignment goes back to the front of the queue (no fallback report)`
17. **Cancelling a subscription input expires its live credit at once; an api-key input's credit stays.**
    - Test: `packages/core/test/bank.test.ts` › `expires its live credit now: replacing a 15M/day donation with 2M/day leaves 2M, not 17M; an api-key's credit stays`

## How it connects

- **Runtime → Bank.** Before a session, `fundingFor` / `fundingForSession` (`packages/runtime/src/session.ts`) picks what pays (invariant 8). While it runs, `bookRunning` debits its usage so far; at its end, `endAndCharge` debits the rest through `Budget.charge` (invariants 1, 2 and 13).
- **Budget → grants.** After every charge, `Budget.afterCharge` runs `Grants.checkpoints` for the building (wired in `packages/core/src/world.ts`). A grant whose spend (summed from its sessions) has reached its tokens is held at its checkpoint. See [grants.md](grants.md), invariant 5.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `a charge that reaches 100% holds the grant once and gives the manager the checkpoint assignment, which may run`
- **Budget → the lane gate.** `Budget.canStart` is the one check the scheduler uses. It checks the credential hold (no credential, or every one limited for the session's model: invariant 15) and the building's remaining budget, then the lane gate (`resolveLane` / `laneBlock` in `packages/core/src/lanes.ts`). The lane gate holds exploration only when no tokens are free under the building's goal or the goal's optional cap is reached (core `packages/core/src/free-tokens.ts`). A held assignment keeps its `heldReason`. Those rules are in [the assignments and lanes invariants doc](assignments-lanes.md).
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `exploration is held with no free tokens, or once its goal's optional cap is reached today; uncapped by default; directed is never held`
