# Invariants: grants

What must always hold in the grants subsystem (`packages/core/src/grants.ts`, the `Grants` service, reached as `world.grantDesk`). Each invariant names the test that proves it. The design is in [docs/how-work-gets-done.md](../how-work-gets-done.md) §4 (older: [docs/goal-directed-budgets.md](../goal-directed-budgets.md)), and the map is in [docs/architecture.md](../architecture.md#map-the-system-in-10-minutes).

**Citation format** (checked by `npm run check:docs`, see `scripts/check-doc-refs.ts`): each invariant has one or more lines like

- Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `a grant carries its own key results under its goal; one with none can't be proposed or funded`

The first code span is the test file. The second is the `it` text, copied exactly. If the file is missing or the text isn't in it, the check fails, so renaming a cited test means updating this doc in the same change.

## Invariants

1. **The status machine moves only along its legal edges.** draft or countered → proposed (propose); proposed → funded, countered or rejected (fund, counter, reject); checkpoint → funded (continue, adjust) or closed (cut); funded → funded (adjust: a human raises the ceiling while it runs); funded or checkpoint → stopping (a human's stop, or cut on a funded grant) → closed (when no session runs on it). Any other status × action pair is refused with a conflict. (`close` withdraws or closes a grant from any status that isn't terminal. Its rules are tested separately.)
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `every grant status × action: only the legal transitions pass`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `close: the manager only as stopped; funders any outcome; proposals can only be withdrawn`
2. **Only a human with `building.fund` (or a member paying from their own Bank input) decides a grant, and agents never hold `building.fund`.**
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `only a human with building.fund decides: counter → edit → propose → fund`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants-schema.test.ts` › `building.fund: admins and human building managers hold it; agents never do, whatever their roles`
3. **A grant's spend is derived from the sessions that carry its `grantId` and is never stored.**
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `spend is derived from sessions; checkpoint decisions continue / adjust / cut`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants-schema.test.ts` › `grants, key results, and the new building/session columns round-trip; spend derives from sessions`
4. **A grant is filed against a goal in its building's subtree and carries 1–5 key results of its own (`key_results.grantId`); one with none can't be proposed or funded** (docs/how-work-gets-done.md §4, migration 069). It sits in the goal tree by its `goalId` alone: no `keyResultId` (migration 126); every grant → goal read goes through `goalId`.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `a grant carries its own key results under its goal; one with none can't be proposed or funded`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants-schema.test.ts` › `migration 069: grants carry their key results; open goal key results with no open grant are dropped`
5. **A grant holds only at its ceiling, and a forecast is filed at every step report** (T&M with a ceiling, docs/legible-delegation.md §7.3). A funded grant holds when its spend reaches grantTokens, never before (the old reserve hold at grantTokens − min(150k, a third) is gone); after an adjust it holds at the new ceiling. The hold fires once per `grantTokens` value, creates no assignment, and while held no new session on the grant starts (running ones finish and book). A grant already held is untouched by `checkpoints()`. Every report of a grant step stores `forecast` on the grant (steps done and left by size, spent, ceiling, spent over size, projected) with `grant.forecast`; a projection past the ceiling also sets `needsTopUp` with `grant.top-up-needed`. The step sizes are priced by the collective's table (`Town.stepSizes`): a proposal's ask is their sum, never the caller's number, and a step marked larger than L is refused.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `the hold fires only at the ceiling and creates no assignment; running work is held, the funders are told (grant C gr_95svkzi4l6)`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `adjust holds at the new ceiling; continue is refused once fully spent; a grant already held is untouched by checkpoints()`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-gates.test.ts` › `a grant holds only at its ceiling: one token short it runs, at the ceiling it holds`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-gates.test.ts` › `every step report files a forecast on the grant: steps done and left by size, spent, spent over size, projected; grant.forecast`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-gates.test.ts` › `a forecast past the ceiling sets needsTopUp and appends grant.top-up-needed with the shortfall; the in-tray row and GET carry it`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/step-sizes.test.ts` › `propose_grant requires a size on every step and refuses XL with the split rule`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/step-sizes.test.ts` › `the ask is the sum of the steps' sizes from the table at propose time; a caller's askTokens is ignored`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `a funded grant already past its hold point when this lands holds at its next charge; a cut closes it`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `a grant task is held while its grant is at checkpoint (stays pending, with a reason); work behind it still starts`
6. **A held grant resumes only with tokens left to spend.** Continue is refused once the grant is fully spent, and adjust is refused unless the new tokens are above the spend.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `spend is derived from sessions; checkpoint decisions continue / adjust / cut`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `refuses adjust to tokens at or below the grant's spend`
7. **Goals keep no open key results.** Every open key result belongs to an open grant: rejecting a proposal archives its key results, `close_grant` marks every open one met (archived), and the goal tree lists only archived ones (history).
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `rejecting a proposal archives its key results; close_grant marks every open one met`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/goals.test.ts` › `the goal tree lists only archived key results; open ones live on grants`
8. **A building's manager sets the status of its grants' key results up to `claimed` (`close_grant`, or PATCH /api/key-results/:id; the checkpoint verb was removed by grant C), and so does a grant-lane fork for its own session's grant, with its report (`submit_report` `keyResults`; invariant 8a). Closing the grant as done marks its open ones met; otherwise only a human who can fund marks one met or missed, undoes that, or deletes it. A key result of an open grant can't be deleted.**
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grants.test.ts` › `a grant's key results: the manager sets status up to claimed, only funders set or undo met / missed; an open grant's can't be deleted`
   - A continue or adjust clears the top-up line (`whatTopUpBuys`, `topUpTokens`); a hold keeps the one the forks filed (test in `packages/core/test/grant-evidence.test.ts`). `GrantView.evidence` (checkpoint grants, and a proposal whose `continues` names an earlier grant of its building; null on a proposal that names none) carries per key result its verdict, note and who set it, `filed` (an agent set one), the grant's own reports (its sessions' reports, oldest first), spent vs granted, the top-up line and a close report's Shipped titles (`packages/core/test/review-deck.test.ts` › `a checkpoint slide leads with what shipped…`).
8a. **A fork files key-result evidence only for its own session's grant.** `submit_report` `keyResults` / `topUp` are accepted only when the session (else its assignment) is in the grant lane, the grant is funded or held, and every key result is an open one of that grant; status is at most `claimed` (schema). A refusal files nothing. On accept each key result gets the status, a note naming the report and `statusBy` = the fork, with a `kr.updated` event; the top-up replaces the grant's.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-evidence.test.ts` › `refuses evidence from another grant's session, an exploration session, a key result of another grant, and a status above claimed`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-evidence.test.ts` › `a grant-lane report sets its own grant's key results (status, a note naming the report, statusBy = the fork) and the top-up`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-evidence.test.ts` › `a hold keeps the top-up the forks filed; an adjust clears it`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-evidence.test.ts` › `evidence() lists the grant's own reports and the forks' notes; 'No evidence filed by the forks' when none; the latest top-up wins`
   - Test: `packages/server/test/grants.test.ts` › `POST /api/verbs/submit_report: a grant-lane fork files its own grant's key-result evidence and top-up; another lane is refused (grant C, kr_13txlz2pnc)`
9. **The owner of a goal (a human) decides and funds grants, and marks key results met or missed, only within the goal's subtree;** admins and `building.fund` holders as before. Agents never fund.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/goals.test.ts` › `owners (humans) inherit down the tree, edit and fund in their subtree only; routing ends at the owner`

10. **Only a human who can fund stops a funded or checkpoint grant, and a stop winds it down before it closes.** `Grants.stop` moves it to `stopping` (not live: no new work, no claim on free tokens), interrupts every fork running on it, cancels its queued work with the reason, archives its open key results as missed with the reason and tells the manager. `finishStopping` closes it as `stopped` only once no session runs on it, recording spent, granted and unspent; a pool-funded rest is free tokens again, a member-funded rest stays on the input. A manager's close as `stopped` of a live grant is refused (403). The stop shows on the Town Hall in-tray (`stopped`, routed, never counted in the badge) and the review deck (a `stopped` slide whose call is None) while it is stopping and for 3 days after it closes.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-stop.test.ts` › `only a human who can fund stops a live grant; the manager's close as stopped on it is refused, its withdraw of a proposal is not`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-stop.test.ts` › `one running fork and one queued assignment: the fork is interrupted, the queued one cancelled with the reason, the grant stopping until the session ends, then closed as stopped`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-stop.test.ts` › `pool-funded with nothing running: closes at once and the unspent rest is free tokens again`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-stop.test.ts` › `member-funded: nothing moves, the rest stays on the member's input and the event says so`
   - Test: `packages/server/test/grants.test.ts` › `stopping a funded grant is a human's: the manager's stop and close-stopped get 403, its withdraw of a proposal 200; a funder's stop closes it and misses its key results (gr_8i952perny)`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-stop.test.ts` › `a stopped grant shows on the Town Hall in-tray and the review deck for the record (who, why, spent and returned), uncounted, for 3 days after it closes`
11. **An agent decides for a human only on the human's own recent words, as that human (`act_in_1on1`, `packages/core/src/act-in-1on1.ts`, gr_kdlogzzo5s).** Only the agent of a discussing 1:1 may act, citing a line of the 1:1's own human from the last 30 min; the decision goes through `Grants.decide` / `decideRetainer` with the human's avatar as actor, so the human's gate applies unchanged. The history entry is the human's (`byId`) with `viaAgentId` / `viaMeetingId` and the note `by <Human> via <Agent> in mt_…: "<their words>"`; event `grant.decided-via`. An open outbox request (dc_…) is declined the same way through `Ops.requestAction` as the human (town.approve_request, as on the button): the request records the human as `requestDecidedById` via the agent and meeting, and the author hears why. Stop, deleting a request and Bank inputs are refused.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/act-in-1on1.test.ts` › `only the 1:1's own human's recent words count; only its agent acts; only while it is open`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/act-in-1on1.test.ts` › `the human's own gate applies: a member without building.fund is refused exactly as the button refuses them`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/act-in-1on1.test.ts` › `refuses stop and Bank inputs, and actions that don't fit the target`
   - Test: `packages/server/test/act-in-1on1.test.ts` › `does what the human said in their 1:1, as them; their gate applies; stop is refused`

12. **A forecast asks nothing; the hold at the ceiling is the only gate** (gates at step reports, docs/legible-delegation.md §7.3 point 4, §8; needs and asks, 1:1 mt_t65exni4et; no forecast asks, 1:1 mt_vdu3iqhasi decision 2). A step report whose forecast projects past the ceiling records it on the grant (`forecast`, `needsTopUp`) and in `grant.forecast` and `grant.top-up-needed`, and writes no need, no ask and no notice: a funded grant runs to its end or its ceiling. A forecast within the ceiling files a `gate` notice for the building and goal ("Step N of M landed: …", refs the grant and the report) and no need. The hold at the ceiling writes the gate's need on the grant's thread (`needAtCheckpoint`, `packages/core/src/asks.ts`): branches Adjust and Cut, and the gate's facts from the grant's history and forecast (the human's prior decision and when, spent against the ceiling, steps done and left by size, projected and the shortfall, the step in flight and its latest report, what each branch does, a suggested branch: adjust by the shortfall rounded up to a whole number of the smallest step size, or cut when the step report said stop or pivot, and the step report). The town writes no card: the manager renders the ask from the need. Adjusting the held grant settles the need, retires the card and keeps the card the human saw on the answer entry. A held grant's need with no forecast and no report suggests nothing it can't back. The projection is recomputed from the step states when the need is written: a step in flight (spend booked, not done) is priced at its size less that spend, never below half the smallest size, so its spend is never counted twice (gr_76e81kdpvy; test `packages/core/test/forecast-asks.test.ts` › the Pins numbers). A boot repair (`retireForecastGates`) retires a gate ask and settles a gate need left open on a grant still funded, citing the decision.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/forecast-asks.test.ts` › `an over-ceiling forecast writes no need and no ask; the forecast and its events are the record`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/forecast-asks.test.ts` › `an on-budget forecast files a gate notice and no need`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/forecast-asks.test.ts` › `the hold at the ceiling writes one gate need with Adjust and Cut and the gate's facts, and no card`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/forecast-asks.test.ts` › `the human's adjust at the hold settles the need and retires the card, keeping the card they saw on the answer entry`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/forecast-asks.test.ts` › `a grant held with no forecast and no report writes its need with what the town knows, and no suggestion it can't back`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/forecast-asks.test.ts` › `the boot repair retires a forecast gate ask and settles its need on a running grant, and leaves a held grant's gate alone`


13. **A key result has one parent and one status** (grant gr_h4m0lyhb1f step 2, migration 125). Its parent is its grant (`grantId`); its building is the grant's (`World.krBuildingId`), and its `goalId` is a copy of the grant's, written only with the grant. Its status is one of open, claimed, met, met-unverified, missed: a fork's report files open or claimed; a human marks met or missed; a close as done picks met or met-unverified; a `kr` claim check the server ran that passes turns a claimed one met (`runClaimChecks`, once, with a `verified` notice). Migration 125 mapped not-started, on-track and at-risk to open, folded the evidence column into the status and dropped `buildingId`, `objectiveId` and `evidence`.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/kr-status.test.ts` › `a fork's report files open or claimed; a human marks it met and can take it back; the checkpoint card shows the status`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/kr-status.test.ts` › `a kr claim check that passes makes a claimed key result met once, with a verified notice; an open one or a failing check leaves it`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/kr-status.test.ts` › `migration 125 folds the old statuses and the evidence column into one status and drops buildingId, objectiveId and evidence`

14. **The Planner never proposes a size's current value, and prices a hold-line raise from a projection** (gr_1ilvotnzta step 1, docs/legible-delegation.md §7.4). `calibrationProposal` (`packages/core/src/planner.ts`) proposes a size outside its 0.7-1.3 band at its median ratio; when hold share or adjust asks cross their line, it raises the hottest S or M (by mean spent ÷ size) by the smallest 10,000 step whose projected hold share (`projectHoldShare`: a seeded bootstrap of the grants funded in 30 days, their real step mixes, done-step spends from `holdSample` in `packages/core/src/grant-estimate.ts`) is at or under the line, capped at the next size's price. A change that would equal the table value, no raise that meets the line, or L running hottest fall back to the split rule. Every proposal carries `holdBefore` and `holdAfter`, and the brief and the adopt line say them. `stepCalibration` gives each size's mean and p75 beside the median.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/planner-calibration.test.ts` › `on today's data it raises S to a new value with the hold share it projects, never 50,000 to 50,000`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/planner-calibration.test.ts` › `never proposes a size's current value: the median-priced raise that rounds to the table falls back to the split rule`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/planner-calibration.test.ts` › `the projection is deterministic, falls as a size rises, and a ceiling margin is an alternative`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/planner-calibration.test.ts` › `stepCalibration reads each size's mean and p75 beside the median; holdSample has the funded grants' step mixes`

## How it connects

- **Budget → checkpoints.** After every charge, `Budget.afterCharge` calls `Grants.checkpoints` (wired in `packages/core/src/world.ts`). That moves a grant whose spend reached its hold point to `checkpoint`, with no assignment (invariant 5).
- **Decisions → assignments.** Fund queues the plan's step 1 on the building queue (`queueStep`, lane grant, `grantStep` 0; no manager session); a step's report with a verdict other than pivot queues the next step (`queueNextStep`), and the manager's review stop or redirect of that report cancels it while it waits. Counter, reject and cut are each the human's entry on the grant's job at level manager (its history entry, written by `packages/core/src/jobs.ts` as the grant is saved; plan dc_uwx045472m step 5): the manager answers it with `review` on the en_ id after making its move (`propose_grant` with grantId answers a counter). No assignment and no town-written report. Fund, continue, adjust and a human's close as done are records on the thread and wake nobody.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `funded: no manager assignment; the plan's step 1 is on the building queue (grant B gr_b5fyqibfx2)`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/grant-steps.test.ts` › `fund puts step 1 on the building queue (lane grant, grantStep 0, its model and pages) and no manager assignment`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `countered and rejected: each is the human's entry on the proposal's job, waiting for the manager; no town-written report, no job (plan dc_uwx045472m step 5)`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/wakes.test.ts` › `a cut waits for the manager as an entry, announced once; a human's key-result edit is a record; the manager's own marks write nothing`
- **Close → key results.** `closeDone` marks each open key result met when its evidence names a live check and met, unverified when it does not (`statusAtClose` in `packages/core/src/kr-evidence.ts`, pure; 1:1 mt_msnjkpgwdb decision 9). Both archive it. No ask is ever filed about a met, unverified key result; a human flips it to met or missed, and only missed is an entry for the manager.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/met-unverified.test.ts` › `a live URL, a notice or event id, or the words that someone saw it live count`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/met-unverified.test.ts` › `a PR, a commit, a code host link, a local address, a doc id or a bare close do not`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/met-unverified.test.ts` › `each open key result is met when its evidence names a live check, else met, unverified; both are archived, and the close entry says which`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/met-unverified.test.ts` › `nobody is asked about it: an ask that targets a met, unverified key result is refused`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/met-unverified.test.ts` › `a human flips it: to met it is verified and wakes nobody; to missed it wakes the manager; an agent can't set or change it`
- **The lane gate reads grant status.** `resolveLane` and `ruleBlock` (`packages/core/src/lanes.ts`) decide whether grant-lane work may start from the grant's status: funded runs, checkpoint holds every new session, and exploration names a goal gap, never a key result. Those invariants live in [assignments-lanes.md](assignments-lanes.md).
