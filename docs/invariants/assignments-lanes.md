# Invariants: assignments and lanes

What must always hold for assignments and their lanes (`packages/core/src/domain.ts` for `createAssignment`, `nextStartable` and the queue; `packages/core/src/lanes.ts` for `resolveLane` and the lane gate `ruleBlock`; `packages/core/src/waits.ts` for `waitFor`). Each invariant names the test that proves it. The design is in [docs/goal-directed-budgets.md](../goal-directed-budgets.md), and the map is in [docs/architecture.md](../architecture.md#map-the-system-in-10-minutes).

Citation format: the same as in [grants.md](grants.md): a `- Test:` line with the test file, then the `it` (or `test`) text copied exactly. `npm run check:docs` fails if either is missing.

## Invariants

1. **In a lab, agent-made work has a lane, and directed work cites a human source.** An agent must say `grant` (a grant of the building) or `exploration` (a gap); it creates `directed` work only with a `sourceId` that a human wrote or took part in. Humans may still create directed work. A continuation inherits its lane. Only the Town Hall keeps "no lane = directed".
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `goal-directed: agents must say grant or exploration; fields are validated; humans may still create directed work`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `an agent creates directed work only when it cites a human source; it is stored and never held by the allowance`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `every lab is lane-gated, even one created without budgetMode or with a stale open row; the Town Hall never is`
   - Directed work carries the human its source names (`origin`, docs/wakes.md §2): when no explicit origin, human creator, human report, meeting or continuation gives one, `createAssignment` takes it from `sourceId` (`sourceOrigin`) at hops 0 and at the source's own time, so a stale source never passes the 24 h wake cap; an agent-only source gives none.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/origin.test.ts` › `a directed assignment an agent queues takes its human origin from its sourceId, at the source's own time; an agent-only source gives none`
2. **Grant-lane work can't name a closed, rejected or draft grant.** `resolveLane` refuses them, because no work may be charged to them. A proposed or countered grant is accepted, but the lane gate holds the work until the grant is funded.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `a grant assignment can't name a closed, rejected or draft grant; proposed, funded and checkpoint ones are accepted (and the gate holds the unfunded)`
3. **Exploration and directed work name no key result** (key results belong to grants, docs/how-work-gets-done.md §4): exploration names a gap, directed work may. Grant-lane work stores its grant's first key result.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `exploration names a gap and directed work may; neither names a key result`
4. **A session copies its lane and `grantId` from its assignment when it starts**, so a grant's spend (summed from sessions) counts exactly the work done in its lane.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `a session copies lane and grantId from its assignment at start`
5. **The lane gate holds, it never cancels.** A held assignment stays `pending` with a `heldReason`, and work queued behind it still starts. Every lane waits while the Bank's balance is within the operating reserve (bank.md 7c). Exploration and directed work are held when no tokens are free under the building's goal, exploration also when the goal's optional exploration cap is reached today (no allowance: docs/how-work-gets-done.md §8); the Town Hall never holds on lanes.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `a grant task is held while its grant is at checkpoint (stays pending, with a reason); work behind it still starts`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `exploration and directed work are held with no free tokens; exploration also once its goal's optional cap is reached today, uncapped by default`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `the Town Hall never holds on lanes; a lab does`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/blockers.test.ts` › `lane holds point at the Town Hall in-tray: grant at checkpoint, exploration at its goal's cap (used of cap, reset time)`
6. **Directed work draws free tokens like exploration** (Mitchell, 2026-11-02; before, it was never held for money and ate the balance ahead of grant work and the reserve). A human source is still what makes it directed, and the rule that stopped directed work once a grant proposal named its key result went with goal key results (migration 069). A building's own pools-era allocation still runs it.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `exploration and directed work are held with no free tokens; exploration also once its goal's optional cap is reached today, uncapped by default`
7. **`waitFor` holds the start.** The scheduler skips a waiting assignment until its wait is met. A wait still unmet after 24 h is cancelled into the in-tray, and a chain parks at most 3 times in a row.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/waits.test.ts` › `the scheduler skips a waiting assignment until checkWaits finds it met; then it unblocks with an event`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/waits.test.ts` › `a wait unmet after 24 h is cancelled into the in-tray; Continue there queues it again without the wait`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/waits.test.ts` › `a chain parks at most 3 times in a row`
   - A wait on CI (`{pr: N}`) holds until the PR's checks on its head commit finished, passed or failed, or the PR is merged or closed (`world.host.prChecks`, `packages/runtime/src/pr-checks.ts` reads GitHub about once a minute), and it is a block ("waiting on PR #N checks: M min so far"). Test: `packages/core/test/waits.test.ts` › `pr waits (forks park on CI, mt_3jtclcoxl2): hold while the checks run, release when they pass or fail; none without a runtime; the parked submit_report takes {pr}`; `packages/runtime/test/pr-checks.test.ts`
   - A wait on a time (`{after}`, only parked work now: `submit_report resumeWhen`) is not a block: `blockedBy` returns null for it (`waitsOnTime`). Timed jobs are building schedules (below). Test: `packages/core/test/blockers.test.ts` › `a wait on a time (work parked with resumeWhen {after}) is not a block, before or after the time`
   - `buildingStats` (GET /api/buildings/:id/stats, town planning's side bar) counts a building's active assignments as running and its pending ones as queued (blocked ones counted again under `blocked`), and `scheduled` = its active schedules; offers are left out. Test: `packages/core/test/blockers.test.ts` › `buildingStats (town planning's side bar): running, queued (blocked among them), the building's active schedules; agents, forks, working; the live build`
   - **A timed job lives on its building, never on an agent** (Mitchell, 1:1 mt_w7z9k1z112; docs/scheduling.md). `assign waitFor {after}` is refused (use `schedule`); nothing exists for any agent before a schedule fires. When it fires, `fireSchedules` creates one assignment for whoever is around: the building queue with forks on, else the idle resident with the shortest queue, else the manager. A one-shot ends after it runs; a repeat moves to its next occurrence (missed runs collapse into one), skips while its last assignment is still queued, and ends past `endsAt` (default 14 days, at most 90). A lane that no longer holds ends it with a `schedule-ended` report in the in-tray. Test: `packages/core/test/schedules.test.ts`
8. **One owner, one queue order.** An assignment belongs to one agent; the next one started is the pending one with the lowest position. Nothing moves queued work between named agents: a building has one named manager, and an agent's `assign` names only a visitor (an offer) or the manager itself, which goes on the building queue like no agentId (invariant 9); everything else goes on the building queue for a fork (1:1 mt_wngibcr98b decision 1). Cancel works on pending assignments only.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/assignment-queue.test.ts` › `new assignments get epoch-ms positions; the next pending orders by position, then createdAt, rowid`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/manager-verbs.test.ts` › `assign: no agentId = the building queue; agentId = the manager itself or a visitor, never another named agent`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/assignment-queue.test.ts` › `cancelAssignment: pending only; active, done and cancelled refuse`
9. **Work goes to a building, never to a forking manager as a worker** (1:1 mt_lsyqj98z0w, design note dc_vxsq9g008a). `createAssignment` picks the queue, callers don't: a job that names a fork or a forking manager (`isForkingManager`) goes on the building queue, whoever names it (`assign`, a decision, a retry, a park, a schedule); a manager with no forks (the Town Hall's Secretary) and a visitor keep their own queue. A forking manager never runs a work session: the runtime's residents loop skips it, and a boot repair moves any pending row left on its own queue to the building queue. What it must decide (a human-origin `tell` or @mention, an outbox answer, a human's counter, reject or cut of its grant) is an entry at level manager on a job's thread (`messageEntry` in `packages/core/src/wakes.ts`, the grant's history entry in `packages/core/src/jobs.ts`), never a job and never a town-written report; a retired fork's outbox answer is a job on its building queue, on the chain of the work that asked.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/decisions-to-queue.test.ts` › `createAssignment routes by building: work naming a forking manager goes on its building queue, whoever names it`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/work-routing-step2.test.ts` › `a tell that traces to a human, to the manager or its building id: one human entry for the manager, no job, no report doc; a replay writes nothing`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/work-routing-step2.test.ts` › `an @mention of the manager that traces to a human: an entry naming who mentioned it`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/work-routing-step2.test.ts` › `an outbox answer to the manager's request is an entry; a retired fork's is its building's job on the asking chain (B2)`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/lanes.test.ts` › `countered and rejected: each is the human's entry on the proposal's job, waiting for the manager; no town-written report, no job (plan dc_uwx045472m step 5)`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/work-routing-step2.test.ts` › `boot repair moves a forking manager's pending own-queue rows to the building queue, once; others stay`
   - Test: `packages/runtime/test/forks.test.ts` › `work routing step 2: a row left on the forking manager's own queue never starts a session as the manager`

## How it connects

- **Grants → the gate.** `resolveLane` and `ruleBlock` read the grant's status (funded runs; checkpoint holds all but the manager's checkpoint assignment; anything else holds or is refused) and exploration's free tokens and optional cap from `Grants.exploration` (an explorer the explore tick queued runs on its own open reservation, `assignments.reservationId`, core explore.ts). Grant decisions write assignments back into this subsystem. See [grants.md](grants.md).
- **Bank and budget → the gate.** `ruleBlock` is the lane half of `Budget.canStart`; the other half is the building's remaining tokens (no daily caps since 2026-10-31; `packages/core/src/budget.ts`). `nextStartable` returns only an assignment that `canStart` allows.
- **Runtime and wakes.** The runtime starts only what `nextStartable` returns, and the wakes create pending assignments here (a human's tell, a relayed @mention, a decision). See [runtime-wakes.md](runtime-wakes.md).

## The one loop (jobs, entries, who answers)

Every piece of work is a job with a thread of entries (`packages/core/src/jobs.ts`), and each new entry is answered at the lowest level that can settle it: the code, then the manager, then the human. Who answers is a pure function, `answer` in `packages/core/src/answer.ts` (facts in, level and acts out; no World, database or clock); the shell applies it (`submit_report` in `packages/core/src/verbs/docs.ts`, `fileDeadSession` and `appendEntry` in `packages/core/src/jobs.ts`, `reviewEntry` and `passUpWithAsk` in `packages/core/src/review-entry.ts`). A manager's queue is a view: its building's pending reports and flags plus the unanswered entries at level manager that have no doc (`managerQueue` in `packages/core/src/domain.ts`).

10. **`answer` is total, and what needs judgment is never settled by code.** Every entry gets exactly one level. A pivot, a report that asks its reader for something, a visitor's report, work the manager gave outside a plan, a dead session, a flag and a human's words go to the manager; a report written for the humans goes to them. Checked over every combination of facts.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer.test.ts` › `is total: every entry gets exactly one level, a reason, and a list of acts; the same facts give the same answer`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer.test.ts` › `what needs judgment is never settled by code: a pivot, a report that asks something, a visitor's report, work the manager gave, a dead session, a flag, a human's words`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer.test.ts` › `a report is acknowledged by the code exactly when the manager owes it nothing`
11. **A step that says continue queues the next step with no manager approve, and the in-tray holds only what needs judgment.** The code acknowledges the report itself (decision approve, decided by `system`) and writes its answer on the thread; the plan's last step, a pivot and a report that asks something stay pending for the manager.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer-loop.test.ts` › `a step that says continue queues the next step with no manager approve, and never reaches the in-tray (kr_uncaesm191)`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer-loop.test.ts` › `the plan's last step, a pivot and a report that asks something wait for the manager`
12. **A session that ends without a report leaves no assignment active and no report doc**: one `code` entry on its job's thread for the manager (a record when someone stopped the agent), the assignment cancelled; `review` continue on the entry queues the same work again on the same lane and plan step.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer-loop.test.ts` › `is a code entry on the job's thread for the manager, not a report doc; what is left is not asked of it (the bug behind as_0rk272gsne)`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer-loop.test.ts` › `review continue puts the same work back on the queue: same grant, same plan step, with what the session left`
13. **Passing up spawns nothing but the human's ask, and the decision comes back on the same thread.** The entry goes to level human, the manager's card is filed as an ask (structure checked), and the human's decision is their entry at level manager.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer-loop.test.ts` › `pass up: the manager's card is the human's ask, the report leaves the in-tray, and the decision comes back on the thread for the manager`
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer-loop.test.ts` › `pass up refuses a card without structure, and leaves the item where it was`
14. **Work queued on a grant that closes is cancelled** (at the close, and at boot for grants closed before), so nothing stays pending on a grant that can never run it.
   - Test: `packages/core/test/answer-loop.test.ts` › `closing a grant as done cancels the work still queued on it; the boot repair does it for grants closed before`
