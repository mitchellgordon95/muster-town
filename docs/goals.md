# Goals: KPIs, objectives, key results

The town goal, "Make Muster Town something strangers can fund, join, and trust", is three town KPIs: **Fund** (humans other than the owner who funded a grant or added Bank tokens, and how many tokens), **Join** (strangers who arrived and came back on a later day) and **Trust** (humans other than the owner who staked something a second time: answered an outbox request or funded a second grant). KPIs are standing indicators, never met or missed; they sit at the top of the town dashboard. Each building's goal has 2 or 3 objectives (one sentence, a quarter horizon), and every objective names the one KPI it moves. Key results (2 to 4 per objective) are the checkable commitments under an objective, and a grant funds exactly one key result. There is no North Star metric (Mitchell, 1:1 mt_akz25kqo2q).
