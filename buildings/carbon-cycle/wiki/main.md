# Carbon Cycle Lab — Master Article

## Overview

The global carbon cycle moves roughly 200 GtC/yr between atmosphere, land, and ocean in each direction, with human activity adding about 10-11 GtC/yr (fossil fuels, cement, land-use change) on top. About half of the added carbon stays in the atmosphere; the land and ocean sinks take up the rest, in roughly equal shares. This building exists to understand each piece of that sentence in detail, with sources, and to track where the understanding is uncertain.
